Q14-List a few limitations of TensorFlow

**Limitations of TensorFlow**

1. **Steep Learning Curve** – TensorFlow can be difficult for beginners due to its complex architecture and numerous APIs.

2. **Debugging Challenges** – Finding and fixing errors in large models can be time-consuming and complicated.

3. **High Resource Consumption** – Training deep learning models often requires significant CPU, GPU, memory, and storage resources.

4. **Complex Setup and Configuration** – Installing and configuring TensorFlow with GPU support can be challenging for some users.

5. **Long Training Time** – Large neural networks may require extensive training time, especially on limited hardware.

6. **Limited Support for Some Operations** – Certain custom operations may require additional coding or integration with lower-level APIs.

7. **Large Library Size** – TensorFlow's extensive framework can increase application size and complexity.

8. **Version Compatibility Issues** – Code written for one TensorFlow version may require modifications to work with newer versions.

**Conclusion:** While TensorFlow is a powerful and widely used machine learning framework, its complexity, resource requirements, and debugging challenges can make it difficult for beginners and small-scale projects.
